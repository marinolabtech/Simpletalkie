SimpleTalkie Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: SimpleTalkie has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your voice and your display name travel directly between the phones in your group. We, the developer, never receive them, and the app does not record or store your audio.

1. Who we are

SimpleTalkie (shown in the app as “Simple Talkie”, package name com.marinolab.simpletalkie) is an Android app developed and published by MarinoLab, an individual developer based in Italy (“we”, “us”). The app works like a group walkie-talkie: you hold a button to talk and the other phones in your channel nearby hear you, without cellular coverage or internet.

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. You can reach us at the address in section 12.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, device identifiers, advertising ID, photos, files or usage statistics.

3. How the information is used

  • Your voice is used only to let the people in your channel hear you in real time.
  • Your display name and channel name are used only to connect and identify the members of the group.
  • The app uses the phone’s call state (through the audio system, without reading your phone number or call history) only to pause automatically during a phone call.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not sell or share it.

4. Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Microphone: to capture your voice while you hold the talk button.
  • Foreground service (microphone) and notifications: to keep the connection and the walkie-talkie working when the screen is off or the app is in your pocket. While the service is active, Android shows a persistent notification with a button to leave the channel.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices (Wi-Fi): to discover and connect to the phones in your group using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. The app declares that Bluetooth scanning is not used to derive your location.
  • Location (Android 12 and earlier only): Android requires this permission for apps that scan for nearby devices on those versions. SimpleTalkie does not read, store or transmit your location.
  • Wi-Fi and network state, wake lock, vibration: to manage the local connections, keep the audio running with the screen off, and give you haptic feedback.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone’s Settings. Without the microphone and nearby-devices permissions the app cannot work.

5. Third-party services

The app connects phones using Nearby Connections, part of Google Play Services. This is provided by Google as part of Android: the connection happens between devices, and the data handled by Google Play Services on your device is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. SimpleTalkie contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

The app also includes buttons that hand over to something outside of it, such as a button to share the app with a friend, which opens the Android share sheet so that you can choose the app to send it with, and a button to support the developer, which opens an external page in your browser or another app. Once you leave SimpleTalkie, the other service’s own privacy policy applies, and we do not receive any information from it through the app.

6. Data stored on your phone and how to remove it

The display name and the channel name are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone. To remove them, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings (Apps, SimpleTalkie, Storage) or uninstall the app. Leaving a channel (the exit button, or “Leave” in the notification) stops the service and the connections.

Features of the operating system, such as device backup, are managed by Android and Google, not by SimpleTalkie. Depending on your device settings, app data on your phone may be included in those system features.

7. Who can hear you

SimpleTalkie is designed for groups that know each other. Channels are identified only by a name, and there is no account or password. Anyone within range who enters the same channel name can join that channel, see the display names and hear the audio. To avoid unwanted listeners, pick a channel name that others are unlikely to guess and share it only with the people you want in the group. Do not say anything in a channel that you would not want the other members to hear. Phones in the group also pass on the audio of other members to extend the range; they do this in real time and do not save it.

8. Security

Because we operate no servers and keep no copy of your data, there is no database on our side that could be breached. The connections between phones are established through Google’s Nearby Connections. No method of wireless transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

SimpleTalkie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone. Because the app lets members of a channel hear each other, we recommend that parents supervise any use by minors.

10. Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that grants data protection rights, you still have the right to ask us about our practices and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Write to us at the address in section 12.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding an internet-based feature, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect, and we will describe the change in the app’s store listing or release notes.
